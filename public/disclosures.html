1. Not a broker-dealer

Wall Street Capital is a technology company, not a registered broker-dealer or investment adviser. We do not provide brokerage services, hold customer funds, or execute trades.

Brokerage services, custody, order execution, clearing, and settlement are provided by our SIPC-member brokerage partner. Your brokerage account is held at that partner, in your name.

2. SIPC coverage

Securities in your brokerage account are protected by the Securities Investor Protection Corporation (SIPC) up to $500,000, of which up to $250,000 may be for cash claims, subject to SIPC rules.

SIPC protection does not protect against market losses. It protects against the failure of a brokerage firm and missing assets held by that firm. SIPC does not protect against fraud committed by you or on your behalf.

Wall Street Capital itself is not SIPC-insured, because it does not hold customer securities.
