Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Wall Street Capital. If your question isn't here, open a support ticket from your dashboard.
Is Wall Street Capital a broker?

No. Wall Street Capital is a technology company. Brokerage services — custody, execution, clearing — are provided by our SIPC-member partner, a registered broker-dealer. We build the interface; they hold the license.

Where is my money held?

At the brokerage, in your name. We never take custody of customer funds. Every deposit and withdrawal is a call to the brokerage's Transfers API — we do not have a balance field in our database that could be incremented.

Are there any account minimums?

No minimum to open an account. You can fund with as little as $1 using fractional shares. Higher account tiers exist for service reasons (see the Plans section on the home page) but they are based on funded balance, not required to trade.

What does it cost?

$0 commission on US equities and ETFs. Regulatory fees and any third-party charges (wire transfers, for example) are passed through at cost.

How do you verify my identity?

Through our brokerage partner. You submit name, address, date of birth, and tax ID. They run the actual CIP/KYC check. We store a status flag indicating whether you've been approved — we do not store your SSN or documents.

How long does verification take?

Most accounts are verified within a few hours. Complex cases can take 1–2 business days. You can track the status on your dashboard while it's in progress.

How do I fund my account?

Link a bank account through Plaid and transfer via ACH. ACH deposits typically settle in 3–5 business days. Wire transfers are available for higher tiers and settle same-day.

Can I withdraw my money at any time?

Yes. There's no lock-up. Withdrawals go back to the same bank account you funded from, subject to standard ACH settlement timelines and any unsettled trades.

Do you offer crypto?

Not currently. This platform is focused on US equities and ETFs. If we add crypto later, it will be through a licensed crypto custodian — not a token we invented.

Is my money insured?

Brokerage accounts at our SIPC-member partner are covered by SIPC up to $500,000 (including $250,000 for cash claims), subject to SIPC rules. SIPC protects against broker failure, not market losses. There is no FDIC insurance because this is not a bank.

What markets can I trade?

US-listed equities and ETFs. Fractional shares are supported, so you can buy any stock from $1. We do not offer options, futures, forex, or crypto at this time.

Can I trade outside of market hours?

Yes. Extended-hours trading is available for pre-market (4:00 AM – 9:30 AM ET) and post-market (4:00 PM – 8:00 PM ET) sessions, subject to liquidity and order-type restrictions.

How do I contact support?

Open a support ticket from your dashboard. Tickets are monitored during business hours. Urgent account issues are prioritised.
